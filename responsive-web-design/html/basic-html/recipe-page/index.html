Turkey Burger & Potato Skillet

If you're looking for a hearty, wholesome meal that delivers big flavor without a sink full of dishes, this Turkey Burger & Potato Skillet is about to become your new weeknight hero. It's high-protein, incredibly satisfying, and comes together in just 30 minutes!

Ingredients

  • 2 turkey burger patties
  • 1/4 yellow onion
  • 1 red potato
  • Personal spice mix

Instructions

  1. Dice the potatoes and cook them in a skillet with oil for 10-15 minues until nearly tender.
  2. Add the sliced onion and cook until softened.
  3. Crumble the turkey burgers into the pan.
  4. Season with your personal spice mix.
  5. Cook until the turkey is browned.

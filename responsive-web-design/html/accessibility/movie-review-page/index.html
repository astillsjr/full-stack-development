Movie Review: The Odyssey

Christopher Nolan's 2026 epic The Odyssey is a gritty, IMAX-shot adaptation of Homer's ancient poem that follows the war-weary king Odysseus on his grueling decade-long journey home to Ithaca after the Trojan War. Utilizing a complex, non-linear structure and minimal CGI to ground its mythological world in reality, the film interweaves Odysseus's perilous trials at sea with the mounting tension in his homeland, where his wife Penelope and son Telemachus fight off aggressive suitors.

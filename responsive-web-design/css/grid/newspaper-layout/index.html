Daily Bugle

Spider-Man Halts Runaway Subway in Midtown

Commuters on the uptown local say a masked figure dropped onto the tracks near 42nd Street and webbed the lead car to a stop before it reached the platform. No injuries were reported. Transit officials credited "an unidentified civilian" and declined to name Spider-Man.

Jameson Demands the Mask Come Off

Daily Bugle publisher J. Jonah Jameson called the subway rescue a publicity stunt and renewed his offer for a photo that unmasks the wall-crawler. "Heroes show their faces," Jameson said. "This city deserves a name, not a slogan."

Web Samples Baffle City Labs

Fibers collected from the Brooklyn Bridge tested stronger than steel cable and dissolved within two hours. The lab still has no source for the material.

Queens Block Party Thanks Its Hero

Neighbors in Forest Hills strung paper webs between stoops after Spider-Man returned a stolen delivery bike. Organizers say the tip jar will go to the local youth center.

Bridge Rescue Floods the Tip Line

Bugle operators logged more than 200 calls after witnesses saw Spider-Man carry two drivers clear of a jackknifed truck. Most asked the same question: who was under the mask?

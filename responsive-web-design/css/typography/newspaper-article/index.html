Breaking: Local Cat Declares Himself Absolute Emperor

Coup d'État Thwarted by the Inconvenient Placement of a Cardboard Box

By Arthur Pendelton

In a shocking development that caught local authorities completely off guard, Barnaby, a notoriously chubby ginger tabby, successfully executed a peaceful takeover of the Johnson household early Tuesday morning, declaring himself the Supreme Sovereign of the Living Room Rug and instituting strict new laws that included mandatory 3:00 AM parkour sessions and an absolute ban on closed doors.

The brief regime faced an unexpected crisis mid-afternoon when Mr. Johnson strategically placed a small, slightly dented shoebox in the center of the hallway, causing Emperor Barnaby to immediately abandon his throne, curl up into a tight loaf formation due to his irresistible urge to sit in a space smaller than his body, and fall fast asleep so that order could be fully restored by the household human servants.
